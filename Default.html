Welcome to RWI Level 1

This tracker is designed to help parents and teachers guide children through the foundational stages of reading using the Read Write Inc. (RWI) phonics programme. Content focuses on Set 1 Speed Sounds.

The Golden Rule: Pure Sounds

It is crucial that sounds are pronounced as "pure sounds". This means stretching sounds like 'm' to 'mmmm' instead of saying the letter name 'em', and keeping bouncy sounds short (saying 't' not 'tuh').

The Set 1 Sound Sequence

Sounds must be taught in this exact, non-alphabetical order to enable early word blending as quickly as possible.

Progression to Reading

Once a child can effortlessly read all Set 1 sounds and blend CVC (consonant-vowel-consonant) words independently using Fred Talk, they are ready for the next step.

Sound Blending Books

Practice blending single words before moving to sentences.

Red Ditty Books

Short, fully decodable passages made up entirely of Set 1 sounds. Read one ditty per day.

Daily Teaching Sequence

Step 1 & 2: Say and Read the Sound

Action Press lips together hard and stretch the sound "mmmmmm"

MTYT "My Turn, Your Turn". Introduce vocabulary.
"Listen: mouse, mountain, mirror... they all start with m!"

Use picture sound cards. Have the child spot the new sound hidden in a pack of previously learned cards.

Step 3 & 4: Review and Write

Quickly review all previously taught sounds using flashcards.

Formation Phrase

"Down Maisie, mountain, mountain"

Teacher Action: Draw the mnemonic picture on the board, then write the letter next to it while saying the phrase clearly. Have the child trace it in the air, then on paper.

Step 5: Speed Write & Fred Talk

Speed Write: Have the child write 3 previously learned sounds as quickly as they can from memory.

Oral Fred Talk

Fred the Frog can only speak in sounds. Orally blend sounds into words.
Teacher says: "m-a-d"
Child blends and says: "mad".
